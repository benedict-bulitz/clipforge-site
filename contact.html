Disconnecting your accounts

If you wish to remove ClipForge's access to your social media accounts, you can do so directly from your account security settings on the respective platforms (TikTok, Instagram, or Google). You can also disconnect an account in ClipForge under Settings → Integrations.

Details on how ClipForge connects to TikTok are on the TikTok Integration page.

TikTok Integration

ClipForge uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API (Direct Post) so that you can post a video you made in ClipForge to your own TikTok account. This page explains exactly how the connection works, which permissions you grant, and what you decide before every post.

Connecting Your TikTok Account

  1. Choose Connect TikTok

    In ClipForge, open Settings → Integrations → TikTok and choose to connect an account.

  2. Sign in on TikTok

    ClipForge opens TikTok's own authorization page. You log in there; ClipForge never sees or stores your TikTok password. The request is protected with PKCE, a standard safeguard for OAuth sign-in from desktop applications.

  3. Review the permissions

    TikTok shows you the permissions ClipForge asks for (listed below). You can approve or cancel.

  4. Return to ClipForge

    After you approve, TikTok returns you to ClipForge, which shows the connected account's display name and profile picture so you always know which account you are posting to. The access credentials are kept in your computer's secure credential storage and are never shown in the interface or written to logs.

Permissions ClipForge Requests

ClipForge requests exactly two TikTok permissions (scopes).

Scope What it allows Why ClipForge needs it
user.info.basic Read your account's basic profile: open ID, union ID, display name and profile picture (avatar). To identify the connected account and show you which TikTok account a post will go to.
video.publish Post a video directly to your TikTok profile, and read the posting options TikTok offers your account (privacy choices, comment/Duet/Stitch settings, maximum video length). To post the video you chose, with the settings you chose, and to offer only the options TikTok allows for your account.

ClipForge does not request access to your TikTok video list, followers, messages, comments or analytics.

What You Decide Before Every TikTok Post

Each time you choose Upload for TikTok, ClipForge asks TikTok for your account's current posting options and builds the post form from them. The upload button stays disabled until every required choice is made.

  • Account: you pick which connected TikTok account to post to.
  • Caption and hashtags: suggested from the video's metadata and fully editable, with a live character count.
  • Who can view this video: chosen from the options TikTok returns for your account. There is no preselected default; you must choose one.
  • Allow comments, Duet and Stitch: each is off until you turn it on, and is unavailable if you have turned it off in your TikTok settings.
  • AI-generated content: you answer whether the video should carry TikTok's AI-generated content label.
  • Commercial content: you can disclose that the video promotes your own brand or is branded content (a paid partnership). Branded content cannot be posted with "Only me" visibility.
  • When: publish now, or schedule a date and time. Scheduled posts are sent by ClipForge only while it is running with internet access; a missed time is never published late on its own.
  • Confirmation: you must confirm "By posting, you agree to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation." before posting.

ClipForge also checks the video against the maximum length TikTok allows for your account and tells you if it is too long.

After You Post

  • ClipForge uploads the final video file to TikTok and shows the post's status: uploading, processing, published or failed.
  • TikTok may need a few minutes to process a video before it appears on your profile.
  • If TikTok rejects a post, ClipForge shows TikTok's own reason and does not retry errors that need your action, such as missing permissions.
  • ClipForge never deletes your TikTok posts.

Visibility Before TikTok's Audit

Until ClipForge's use of the Content Posting API has passed TikTok's audit, TikTok only allows posts with "Only me" (private) visibility. ClipForge never silently changes the visibility you choose: if a public option is not yet allowed, it is shown as unavailable and you choose "Only me" yourself.

Disconnecting TikTok

  • In ClipForge: disconnect the account in Settings → Integrations. ClipForge deletes the stored TikTok credential from your computer and asks TikTok to revoke its access. If TikTok cannot be reached, the stored credential is still deleted.
  • In TikTok: you can also remove ClipForge's access at any time in your TikTok account's security and app-permission settings.
  • What ClipForge keeps: the account's identity record (marked as disconnected) and the history of posts made to it. Posts already scheduled for the account are not cancelled automatically, but they are not published while it is disconnected. Cancel them in ClipForge if you no longer want them. Details are in the Privacy Policy under Disconnecting an Account.

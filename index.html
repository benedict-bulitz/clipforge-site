Create and Publish Short-Form Videos

ClipForge helps you automate your short-form video workflow. It turns one idea into a finished short video, then lets you upload or schedule it on your own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. Nothing is published until you choose to publish it.

From Idea to Video

Start from a single question or idea. ClipForge writes a compact script, plans the visuals, captions and music, and renders a finished short video (vertical 9:16 by default) that you can refine in plain language.

Supported Platforms

Connect and publish to popular networks including YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok, all from one place, using each platform's official API and sign-in.

You're in Control

ClipForge does not claim ownership of your connected accounts. Users retain full control over what gets published and when.

What ClipForge Is

ClipForge is a desktop application for macOS. It runs on your own computer, and its interface opens in your web browser. It combines two jobs that usually need separate tools: producing a short informational video, and publishing that video to the social accounts you connect.

How Video Creation Works

Every video starts as a project. Each change is saved as a new revision, so earlier versions are never lost.

  1. Start with one idea

    Type a question or idea in plain language, or pick one of ClipForge's topic suggestions. Your original prompt is kept unchanged with the project.

  2. ClipForge plans the video

    It decides whether research is needed, keeps only the essential information, writes a compact script, estimates the natural duration, and builds a storyboard of visual beats with captions, music and a timeline.

  3. Refine it in chat

    Ask for changes in your own words. ClipForge applies the change as a new revision, and you can undo it at any time.

  4. Render the final video

    ClipForge renders a finished MP4 with narration, captions timed to the spoken words, and music. You can save it locally or publish it.

How Social Publishing Works

Publishing always starts with you. Every platform receives the same final video file, and you review each platform's settings before anything is sent.

  1. Connect your own accounts

    In ClipForge's settings, you connect the YouTube, Instagram or TikTok accounts you manage. Each connection uses the platform's official sign-in (OAuth): you log in on the platform's own page and approve the permissions there. ClipForge never sees your password.

  2. Open the Upload sheet

    After a video is rendered, choose Upload and pick the account. ClipForge shows only the settings that platform supports, such as the caption, visibility and interaction options.

  3. Review and confirm

    You edit the caption and hashtags, choose who can see the post, and answer the required disclosures. The upload button stays disabled until every required choice is made.

  4. Publish now or schedule

    Publish immediately, or pick a date, time and time zone. YouTube schedules natively. For Instagram and TikTok, ClipForge sends the post when it is due, but only while ClipForge is running with internet access. A post whose time passes while your computer is off is marked Missed and waits for you to publish, reschedule or cancel it.

  5. Follow the result

    ClipForge shows each post's status (for example scheduled, uploading, processing, published or failed) with the platform's own reason when something fails. Removing a video from ClipForge's library never deletes the post on the platform.

Supported Platforms

ClipForge requests only the permissions it needs to publish your videos and to show which account is connected.

Platform What ClipForge does Sign-in and permissions
TikTok Posts your video with the caption, visibility, interaction settings and disclosures you choose. Details TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API (Direct Post): user.info.basic, video.publish
YouTube Uploads your video with title, description, tags, thumbnail, audience and visibility settings, optionally scheduled, and reads analytics for videos uploaded through ClipForge. Google OAuth: youtube, yt-analytics.readonly
Instagram Publishes your video as a Reel with your caption. Requires an Instagram professional (Business or Creator) account linked to a Facebook Page you manage. Facebook Login for Business: instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement

You Stay in Control

  • No automatic posting without you. ClipForge does not post any content without your direct instruction or a schedule you created.
  • Your own accounts only. ClipForge publishes only to accounts you connected yourself, and you choose the account for every post.
  • Credentials stay protected. Access tokens are kept in your computer's secure credential storage and are never shown in the interface or written to logs.
  • Disconnect any time. You can disconnect an account in ClipForge's settings or revoke access in the platform's own security settings.
